# AI Jobs & Salaries Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes AI-related job salaries and explores how salary varies based on experience level, job title, work mode, company size, location, and year.

The goal of this project is to understand salary patterns in the AI and data industry using SQL and Python-based data analysis.

---

## Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze AI job salary distributions
- Compare salaries across experience levels
- Identify the most common AI job titles
- Compare average salaries across different job roles
- Analyze salary differences by work mode
- Analyze salary differences by company size
- Explore salary differences across company locations
- Identify salary outliers
- Find important patterns and insights from the dataset

## Key Findings

### Overall Salary

- Total job records: **71,913**
- Average salary: **$151,161**
- Median salary: **$138,750**
- Minimum salary: **$15,000**
- Maximum salary: **$800,000**

The difference between the mean and median indicates that high salaries influence the overall average.

### Salary by Experience Level

| Experience Level | Job Count | Average Salary |
|---|---:|---:|
| Executive (EX) | 577 | $206,724.72 |
| Senior (SE) | 5,636 | $171,146.14 |
| Mid-Level (MI) | 3,445 | $135,062.39 |
| Entry-Level (EN) | 717 | $99,781.75 |

Executive-level positions have the highest average salary, while entry-level positions have the lowest.

### Most Common Job Titles

| Job Title | Job Count | Average Salary |
|---|---:|---:|
| Software Engineer | 905 | $182,812.32 |
| Data Engineer | 859 | $141,699.82 |
| Data Scientist | 801 | $158,591.55 |
| Engineer | 766 | $163,854.40 |
| Data Analyst | 719 | $102,550.45 |

Software Engineer is the most common job title among the listed top roles and also has a high average salary.

### Salary Outliers

The dataset contains **1,754 flagged salary outliers**.

These unusually high salaries can significantly affect average salary calculations, which is why both mean and median salary were considered.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Experience level should be considered an important factor when evaluating AI industry salaries.
2. Professionals should compare both job role and experience level when evaluating career opportunities.
3. Salary comparisons should consider location and company characteristics.
4. Median salary should be considered alongside average salary because extreme salaries can affect the mean.
5. Companies can use salary analysis to benchmark compensation and improve competitive hiring strategies.

## Conclusion

This project analyzed **71,913 AI-related job records** to understand salary patterns across experience levels, job titles, work modes, company characteristics, locations, and years.

The analysis shows a clear relationship between experience level and salary, with executive-level roles having the highest average compensation and entry-level roles having the lowest.

The project demonstrates a complete data analysis workflow using **SQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**, from data understanding and analysis to visualization and business insights.
